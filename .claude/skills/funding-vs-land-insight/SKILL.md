# Funding vs land insight

1. Pull for the area: funding by year and category (2021+), and the headline land metric for its study_type (tree cover loss for basin/rural, built-up or green space for urban).
2. Compare only overlapping years. State the window explicitly.
3. For basins, split spending and land change into upstream and downstream.
4. Write 3 to 5 sentences in this order: what changed on the land, how much was spent and on what, where the spending sits relative to the change, data caveats.
5. Language rules:
   - Use: "in the same period", "mostly downstream", "for review", "pattern", "mismatch".
   - Never use: "corruption", "ghost project", "proves", "caused by", "deforestation" (for Hansen).
6. Always include caveats: DPWH covers 2021+ and only DPWH, project coordinates are sites not benefit areas, tree cover loss includes storms and fires.
7. No em dashes.

Example:
"From 2021 to 2024, the upland part of this area lost about X ha of tree cover. In the same period, 168 DPWH flood control projects worth ₱7.7B were recorded here, mostly drainage and river structures downstream. No watershed or reforestation spending appears in this dataset, which covers DPWH only. This pattern is worth review alongside DENR and LGU records."
